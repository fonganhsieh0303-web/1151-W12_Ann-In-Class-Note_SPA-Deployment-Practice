1.1 The 4-step analysis process1.1 四步驟分析流程

1

Separate拆解

Break the whole into Σ parts / components; note their potential relationships. Break down the problem, the demand and the questions.將整體拆解為各部分／元件，並留意其潛在關係；拆解問題、需求與提問。

2

Investigate deeply深入探究

Use evidence (e.g., concrete examples).以證據佐證（例如具體實例）。

→
3

Recombine重組

Put the components back together based on their relationships.依各元件間的關係重新組合。

→
4

New representation → Insights新的呈現 → 洞見

Present the result in a new form (e.g., a Coggle mind map).以新形式呈現結果（例如 Coggle 心智圖）。

2

From Demand to Product從需求到產品

2.1 Market logic2.1 市場邏輯

Producers生產者

(supply)（供給）

→
Market市場 product ⇄ value產品 ⇄ 價值
←

Consumers消費者

(demand)（需求）

  • Producers offer a product; consumers bring value; money ($) flows from consumers back to producers.生產者提供產品，消費者帶來價值；金流（$）由消費者回流至生產者。
  • Value = WTP (willingness to pay), defined for the TA (target audience).價值 = WTP（願付價格），依TA（目標受眾）界定。
  • Product decisions must be backed with evidence (see 1.1 step 2).產品決策須以證據佐證（見 1.1 步驟 2）。

Key relationships關鍵關聯

The analysis steps match the AI pipeline: separate/investigate = collect & analyze; recombine = summarize; new representation = SVG / SPA.分析步驟對應 AI 流程：拆解／探究 = 蒐集與分析；重組 = 摘要；新的呈現 = SVG／SPA。

Evidence supports both thinking (mind map) and business decisions (market).證據同時支撐思考（心智圖）與商業決策（市場）。

PRD appears twice: as a business concept (Part 2) and as a project file, PRD.md (Part 3).PRD 出現兩次：作為商業概念（第 2 部分）及專案檔案 PRD.md（第 3 部分）。

Front-end / Back-end (Part 2) is what gets deployed in Part 3.前端／後端（第 2 部分）即第 3 部分所部署的內容。

Effective over efficient: first make sure the product or summary reaches its purpose.有效能優先於有效率：先確保產品或摘要達成目的。

This SPA itself is Part 3, Step 5 (Visualize / Build), applied to this week's note.本單頁應用程式本身即第 3 部分步驟 5（視覺化／建置）的實作。
